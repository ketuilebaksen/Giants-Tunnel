Now let’s go straight to the source, because John Harbaugh sat down this week on his weekly show and gave us a fascinating look inside the Arizona game, and it tells you everything about this team’s culture. Harbaugh said the biggest thing that stood out was how the defense handled adversity early. The run defense was not doing a good job at the start. Arizona got them with a couple of schemes, and the Giants did not play them well. They had to make a couple of adjustments in the way they were lining guys up. And here is the line, folks. Nobody panicked. Nobody got upset. Everybody just talked it through, and they got it figured out.

And then Harbaugh walked through what happened after the adjustment, and it is a beautiful stretch of football. An interception on a two-play drive. Then three straight three-and-outs. Then another interception. Then another three-and-out. Harbaugh said it himself, that is a lot of great defense in a row. Folks, think about that. A defense that was getting gashed early on the ground, with a team that had jumped out to a 14 to 3 lead, sat down on the sideline, talked it through like professionals, made the fix, and then went on a run of six straight stops and turnovers. That is not talent alone. That is coaching, communication, and maturity.

Of course, it was not perfect, and Harbaugh was honest about that too. Arizona hit a pass and kicked a field goal at the end of the half. Harbaugh said, we would like for the game to be over, but it is not. And then the pass interference call put Arizona at the four-yard line in the final seconds.
