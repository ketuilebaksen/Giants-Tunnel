John Harbaugh just stepped to the podium before the Saints game, and he made two announcements that every Giants fan needs to hear. First, the J.J. McCarthy package is not done. Harbaugh said it plainly. What you saw is probably not all we have for him right now. Second, Abdul Carter, the young star who has been carrying this pass rush since Brian Burns went down, was not seen at practice, and Harbaugh confirmed he will be on the injury report. Folks, those two lines alone are enough to change how you think about Sunday. But Harbaugh did not stop there. He defended Kayvon Thibodeaux and told the media they are underestimating him. He explained exactly how this staff is adapting the offense to Jameis Winston. He revealed what went wrong with Dominic Zvada’s kicks. He called the fumbles a major problem that is still not good enough. And he gave us a fascinating look at why Isaiah Likely is exploding this season. Today I am going to walk you through the whole press conference, quote by quote, and tell you what I think every answer really means. Let’s get into it.

Harbaugh opened the press conference the way he usually does, setting the tone. He said, beautiful day, good tough practice. The guys did well, and we are pointing toward the Saints game on Sunday. We are looking forward to it. Big challenge. And then he said something I want you to remember. He said he is anticipating a very hungry team coming in, a very talented team, and a very well-coached team. Folks, that is classic Harbaugh. He is not going to let his players think for one second that this is an easy game, even at home, even at MetLife where they have not lost all season. He is building respect for the opponent before the week even gets going. And honestly, that is exactly what a 3 and 1 and 1 team needs to hear after a weird tie.

Now, the first big headline. A reporter pointed out that Abdul Carter was not seen at practice and asked how he is doing. Harbaugh’s answer was short. He said, he is doing well, doing very well. And when pressed on whether it was an injury, he said, he will be on the injury report, status will come out at four. Then, with a little smile, he said, appreciate it, it is a great question. Folks, that is Harbaugh being Harbaugh. He is not going to give away anything before the official report. But here is how I read it. When a coach says a player is doing very well, that is usually a good sign. It does not sound like panic. But the fact that Carter was not seen on the field and will be on the report means we have to watch this closely. Carter has been the engine of this pass rush. A sack and a forced fumble in Washington, chasing Jayden Daniels down from behind. If he is limited on Sunday, Dennard Wilson’s blitz-heavy approach becomes even more important, and players like Kayvon Thibodeaux, Jadeveon Clowney, and Andru Phillips will have to carry more of the load. I will be watching that injury report like a hawk.

Now, the second headline, and to me, this is the biggest one. A reporter asked Harbaugh about the benefits of the package with J.J. McCarthy. And Harbaugh said this. The benefit of it is that it is a change of pace. What you saw is probably not all we have for him right now. Whether or not he is out there will be determined based on whatever happens, but we will see if it is something that can help us. Folks, read that again. What you saw is probably not all we have for him. That means the McCarthy package we saw in his quiet debut in Washington was just the beginning. There is more in the playbook. More designed plays, more ways to use his legs, more ways to stress a defense. And with Breece Hall now in the backfield, think about what a read-option look with McCarthy and Hall could do.

And then Harbaugh gave a line that I think explains his entire coaching philosophy. He said, what is the benefit of not doing everything you can to use all the guys you have? We are always going to be searching for opportunities to use our players and give them an opportunity to help us win games. I think we would be negligent if we did not try to do that. Negligent, folks. That is a strong word. Harbaugh is telling you he views it as a coaching failure to leave talent on the bench. So whether it is McCarthy in a package, Theo Johnson on special teams, or a nickel corner blitzing off the edge, this staff is going to use every player it can. That is the mindset of a team trying to win now.

Now let’s talk about Jameis Winston, because Harbaugh’s answer here was really honest, and I loved it. A reporter asked what he has seen from Winston since he got thrown into the fire. Harbaugh said, it is tough to just kind of jump in there when the offense is built probably for a different quarterback. So I think he did a really good job, but it was really tough. It was awkward, and he fought through it, and he kept his confidence, and he kept his poise, and he found a way to win football games. And now we are starting to get adapted, I think, a little bit to the game plan and what we are doing, and getting reps on the things that are more Jameis-type things. So I think we are going to keep getting better. That is the plan.

Folks, this is huge. For weeks on this channel, we have been talking about the scheme problem. The offense was built for Jackson Dart, with bootlegs, run-pass options, and designed movement. Winston was trying to run an offense that did not fit him. And now the head coach is openly admitting it. He used the word awkward. And then he told us the solution, they are getting reps on Jameis-type things. That is the shotgun, the tempo, the quick passing game we saw take over in the second half in Washington. When the head coach says, we are going to keep getting better, that is the plan, he is telling you the offense is being rebuilt around the quarterback who is actually playing. That is exactly what we have been asking for.

And then a reporter asked how much of this is the coaches adapting to Winston, and how much is Winston adapting to the coaches. Harbaugh said, probably both are required. But a player is kind of what he is. Although he is willing to do anything, and he is probably more versatile than maybe some people give him credit for. He can do a lot of things. You saw him scramble, make all those scramble plays. And then Harbaugh shared a story from the team meeting. He said they were having fun, and Jameis said, you know, I have always been a scrambler. Folks, I love that. You can picture the room laughing. But there is truth in it too. Winston made some big plays with his legs in Washington, extending drives and throwing on the run. He is not Lamar Jackson, but he is more mobile than people think. And Harbaugh is telling you the coaches see that.

Then came the question about the Jameis Winston experience, the nickname everybody uses. A reporter said sometimes it feels like there is a negative connotation to it, because of the interceptions. Harbaugh responded right away. Oh, that would not be a negative connotation. And then he said, I try not to judge other people’s opinions too much. Folks, that is a head coach protecting his quarterback. He is not going to let the media turn Winston into a punchline. The Jameis Winston experience, in that building, is a positive thing. And after a game with 326 yards, three touchdowns, and zero interceptions, why would it not be?

Now, the kicker. A reporter asked how you coach a young kicker like Dominic Zvada, who was great all summer and then struggled in Washington. Harbaugh explained that there are two tracks. First, the technique. He said, the thing you coach the most is the technique. It is like any other position. There is a technique to it, and when you watch the misses, the technique is not the same as the makes, and you can see it, and you just go through that. Second, he said, you figure out why it happened, talk it through, and make the correction going forward.

And here is the part that made me smile. Harbaugh said special teams coordinator Chris Horton handled it, and that he did not even need to get involved this time. And he said Dom already knew. He said Zvada actually knew what he did before the game was even over. When a reporter asked what the technique difference was, Harbaugh said it had to do with his plant foot. And then he joked, that is not going to go through kicking technique, are we? You know better than that. I feel like you are testing me. The room laughed. Folks, that tells you two things. First, it confirms what we said last week. On that torn-up, muddy field in Washington, Zvada could not plant his foot properly. That is a conditions issue, not a talent issue. Second, it tells you the young kicker is self-aware. He knew what went wrong immediately. That is the kind of kicker who bounces back. And remember, this is a man who was perfect before Washington and scored every Giants point against Tennessee. I am not worried.

Now, my favorite moment of the whole press conference. A reporter asked if Kayvon Thibodeaux is being asked to do something different than Abdul Carter, or if the team just needs more from him. And Harbaugh pushed back hard. He said, I think you guys are underestimating what Kayvon is bringing to the table, and the plays that he is making, and the contribution he is making. Everybody wants to talk about stats. Stats are important. They are production. That is a big thing. But the tape is what tells the story. If you study the tape, you will see a lot of really good plays, a lot of very physical plays, and a lot of good football plays, and production will come off of that.

And then Harbaugh brought up the final play in Washington. He asked the reporters, how about the play at the end? You know whose play was that? Pretty darn good play. That is not in the stat sheet, though, is it? He took that throw away, and the quarterback decided to tuck it. Folks, we talked about this exact play on this channel last week. Thibodeaux took away the tight end, stayed disciplined on Jayden Daniels, and forced him back into the middle, where the clock ran out. That play saved the Giants from a loss. And it does not show up anywhere in the box score. Harbaugh is telling the whole league, stop judging Thibodeaux by his sack total. And honestly, after watching that tape, I agree with him. Thibodeaux is in a contract year, and his numbers have not been flashy, but he is setting the edge, playing physical, and making winning plays. Harbaugh said it simply. He is playing very well.

Next, the offensive line. With right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor hurt, a reporter asked about the two young tackles who could be pressed into action as the swing tackle. Harbaugh said, I have seen a lot of work, a lot of diligent work. They are both very good players who are trying to prove themselves in this league. If and when they get a chance to play, I am sure they will be very determined to play well. So we will see. Folks, that is a coach who is not overpromising, but who believes in his depth. And remember, rookie first-round pick Francis Mauigoa has already been lining up with the starting line. The Giants have bodies up front. The question is who steps into Eluemunor’s spot if he cannot go.

Then the fumbles, and Harbaugh did not dodge this one at all. A reporter asked how much ball security has been a point of emphasis and whether it is even something you can coach. Harbaugh said, you can definitely coach it. It is a major point of emphasis. The league is more of a punch-out league than it has ever been before, and more players are really good at it than they have ever been before. And we better ramp it up just like everybody else in the league. It has been a major point of emphasis from day one, and it is still not good enough. So we have got to get a lot better in that area. That has probably been the most important thing with our offense right now. The fact that we have had a number of fumbles, way more than we should, we are making a big deal out of it.

Folks, that is as direct as Harbaugh gets. Way more than we should. Still not good enough. We are making a big deal out of it. The Giants lost two fumbles in Washington, one from Winston and one from Cam Skattebo in overtime, and either one of those could have flipped a tie into a win. This is the number one thing that has to be fixed against the Saints. And I love that Harbaugh is not hiding from it. He is putting it right in front of his players and the cameras.

Then a reporter asked about the standings, whether it is too early to look. Harbaugh said, you always know what they are, but you have got to stack wins to make the December standings matter. So we focus on the next game. Folks, that is the message he has been preaching since September. Win the next game. The Giants are 3 and 1 and 1 and alone in first place, but Harbaugh does not care about October standings. He cares about December. Stack wins now, and the standings take care of themselves.

Now, the Isaiah Likely section, because this was fascinating. A reporter pointed out that Harbaugh drafted Likely in Baltimore, and asked what makes him such a great target, especially with Winston over the last two games. Harbaugh said, he is a really unique kind of athlete. When he was being evaluated, he went in the fourth round, I think it was the late fourth round. Nobody really could get their arms around him. He made all these plays in college, but it just looked different. And you see it out here. Turns out it is really good, what he does. He is elusive. He can get away in man and zone coverage. He has got a great feel. He has got great hands. He has an amazing catch radius. And he is an elusive runner, a good strong runner.

And then Harbaugh added a detail I had never heard. He said that in college at Coastal Carolina, Likely was crazy good as a perimeter blocker. His words, he would launch guys through the air, like five yards through the air sometimes at Coastal. And he said Likely is a really good perimeter blocker here too. So he has translated really well, and he has been able to express what kind of player he is this year. Folks, Likely just became the first tight end in Giants history with nine catches and two touchdown catches in a game. He had eleven catches for 94 yards and two scores in Washington.

And when a reporter asked whether Likely’s breakout is because he has progressed, because the Giants are using him more, or because he was stuck behind Mark Andrews in Baltimore, Harbaugh said, honestly, all of the above. He made lots of great plays in Baltimore, but he did not have as many opportunities there, and those reasons were fine, because Mark Andrews is a great player as well. Folks, that is the story of Likely in one answer. A talented player who was waiting for his chance. Harbaugh brought him to New York and gave it to him. And now he is on pace to blow away his career numbers.

And speaking of tight ends, there was a great answer about Theo Johnson. Last season, Johnson had zero special teams snaps. This season, he has been all over the kickoff coverage unit. Harbaugh said Theo has been adamant about wanting to be out there. When he is running down on kickoff coverage, he is running people over and making tackles. It has been awesome to see. And then Harbaugh joked, I would not want to stand in front of him trying to block him on a kickoff return. Folks, a starting-caliber tight end volunteering for special teams is exactly the kind of buy-in that builds a winning culture. And Harbaugh added a fun note. He said Arvell Reese would be a great special teams player on all four phases, but he is playing so many snaps on defense that it is hard to justify it. That tells you how valuable Reese has become.

And finally, Harbaugh talked about the Saints quarterback, Tyler Shough, who came out in the same 2025 draft class as Jackson Dart. Harbaugh pointed out that Shough has been among the league leaders in passing yards since he became a starter, and that his top receiver, Chris Olave, is right there among the receiving leaders. He said, those two guys are a connection right now. Folks, that is the challenge Sunday. A hungry Saints team with a young quarterback and a receiver who are clicking. Dennard Wilson’s blitz packages, the secondary with Deonte Banks, Greg Newsome, and Joey Porter Jr., and whoever is available on the edge will have to slow down that connection.

So let’s put it all together. The McCarthy package has more in it than we have seen. Abdul Carter is on the injury report, but Harbaugh says he is doing very well. The offense is being rebuilt around Jameis-type things, and Harbaugh openly admitted the early weeks were awkward. Zvada’s misses were about his plant foot on a torn-up field, and he already knows what to fix. Thibodeaux is better than the stat sheet says, and Harbaugh pointed to the play that saved the Washington game. The fumbles are the number one problem, and the head coach is making a big deal out of it. And Likely is finally getting the opportunity he never had in Baltimore.

Folks, this is what a well-run team sounds like. Honest about the problems. Confident in the players. Focused on the next game. I keep saying it, and after listening to this press conference, I believe it even more. This team can go all the way this season.
