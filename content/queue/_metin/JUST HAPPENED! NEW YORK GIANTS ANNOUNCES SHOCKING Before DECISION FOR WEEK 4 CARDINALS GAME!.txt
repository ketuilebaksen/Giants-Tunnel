And the connections run deeper than most fans realize, folks. Dennard Wilson, the defensive coordinator calling this defense, was in Tennessee when Jihad Ward played there. Ward also spent multiple seasons playing for John Harbaugh in Baltimore. So the coach knows him, the coordinator knows him, and he knows exactly what the expectations look like in a Harbaugh program. That familiarity is worth its weight in gold for a midseason addition, because there is no onboarding period, no culture adjustment, no learning curve on the standards. He can be useful on Sunday, not in November. And before anybody shrugs at the name, let me remind you what this man did in Tennessee in 2024, because the numbers are better than you remember. He finished that season with 31 tackles, four tackles for loss, 47 pressures, and 22 quarterback hits, with career highs across several of those categories. Forty-seven pressures, folks. For a defense that entered last Sunday with one sack as a team, a guy who affects the quarterback forty-plus times a season is not a courtesy signing, he is a direct answer to the problem.
