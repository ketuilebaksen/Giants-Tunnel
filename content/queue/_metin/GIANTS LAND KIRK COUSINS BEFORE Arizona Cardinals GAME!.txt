He reads the field fast, he throws on time, and he does not give the football away. He is not going to make magic with his legs, he is not escaping three defenders and throwing fifty yard dimes off his back foot. He is going to run the offense exactly as it is drawn, take the checkdown, hit the play action window, and let the playmakers play.
